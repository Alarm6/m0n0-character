# Реалистичная архитектура — вторая редакция

Создано встроенным ImageGen. Исходные варианты сохранены отдельно; сайт использует файлы `dist/assets/character-*-real.png`.

Основной вид: две прямоугольные жилые башни с регулярной сеткой окон, светлым камнем, бронзовыми рамами, оливковым кирпичом и четырёхэтажным стилобатом. Референс пользователя использован для архитектурной достоверности. Соблюдена тёплая палитра сайта. Без органических лент и фантазийных консолей.

## realNight

Use case: lighting-weather. Change ONLY the time of day of the supplied photograph to believable early dusk with muted grey blue sky and a scattering of warm interior window lights (NOT every window illuminated), discreet realistic courtyard lighting. Preserve EXACT camera framing, architecture, window counts, materials, trees, paths, benches. No blue facade, no fantasy lighting. Restrained beige stone, bronze, muted olive palette. No text or watermark.

## realGarden

Use case: ads-marketing. Same residential complex as reference: strict rectangular limestone towers, regular vertical dark bronze windows, four storey podium, muted olive brown brick portions. New realistic courtyard-level photograph looking along stone walking paths toward entrance podium, with portions of the two towers in background. Real birch trees, modest grasses, simple benches, an understated ground-floor cafe terrace, believable planting density. Natural overcast warm morning light, documentary architectural photography, material joints and wear, no perfect CGI gloss, no rounded futuristic terraces, no excessive greenery, no text or watermark. Wide landscape 16:9.

## realTower

Use case: background-extraction. Isolate the taller RIGHT tower and its podium from the supplied image as an architectural object on a truly transparent background. Preserve strict rectangular geometry, limestone panel joints, vertical bronze windows, recessed loggias and modest podium planting. Show entire building roof to ground in three quarter perspective, portrait centered framing. Remove sky, all other buildings and foreground trees, all surrounding ground. Believable completed apartment tower, not futuristic, same restrained beige bronze olive palette. No text, no logo, transparent background.
